(02)HOW I WORK

Calm screens come
from loud thinking.

Three habits I bring to every product — whether it’s a loan journey, an HR suite or a kids’ coding platform.

01

Start with the why

Before pixels, I talk to the people who use it. Interviews, usability tests and the uncomfortable ‘why’ decide what the screen needs to do.

02

Make it obvious

Hard flows get broken into small, clear steps. If a user has to think about the interface, the interface isn’t done yet.

03

Ship, then listen

Systems and tokens first so teams can move fast — then I watch the numbers and the people, and iterate.
