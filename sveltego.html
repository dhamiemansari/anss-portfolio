✦ ABOUT THE PROJECT

Online fashion asks you to buy without trying. svelteGo brings the fitting room home.

SvelteGo is a mobile-first fashion app that helps people shop with confidence. It tackles sizing doubts, return hassles and endless choice with a “Try at Home” experience — curated outfits from personal stylists, delivered to the door, paid for only if they’re kept.

✦ THE PROBLEM

What’s broken in
online fashion?

01Fit is a guess

Shoppers can’t tell how clothes will fit, feel or look before buying.

02Returns pile up

Expectations don’t match reality — so return rates stay high.

03Too much choice

An endless catalogue leads straight to decision fatigue.

04No human touch

Recommendations feel generic; there’s no stylist in the loop.

05No try-on confidence

People miss the “try before you buy” certainty of a store.

✦ THE SOLUTION

Three promises, one fitting room.

The “try before you buy” gap, closed with three ideas — and the onboarding says exactly that, one screen each.

01Try at Home

Order a few outfits, try them in your own mirror, pay only for what you keep.

02Curated outfits

Personalised picks built around your preferences, size and style.

03Stylist assistance

Book a stylist for a virtual or in-person session when you want a second eye.

DOES

Visits the app and signs up.

Explores categories, adds outfits to a trial cart, picks sizes.

Confirms the selection and schedules a free home trial.

Receives the clothes and tries them on at home.

Decides what to keep and what to return.

THINKS

“Is this service available in my area?”

“Will this size fit? Should I try two?”

“I hope delivery is on time and returns are easy.”

“These fit! But one needs a different size.”

“Do I really need this? Any discounts?”

PAIN

A complex sign-up may cause drop-offs.

Confusion about sizing, images and trial limits.

Fear of delays and difficult return policies.

Some items may not fit or suit.

Hesitation over price or choice.

OPPORTUNITY

Fast social sign-up and clear service-area info.

AI size help, real reviews and model size references.

Same/next-day delivery, clear returns, live tracking.

Easy size re-order without a full return.

Personalised offers to nudge the decision.

UI 01ONBOARDING & SIGN-UP

Login in seconds,
no password to forget.

Enter a mobile number, get an OTP, you’re in — Google sign-in as a fallback. A first-order offer sits right above the form, so the reason to sign up is never in doubt.

Login

Mobile number first; Google as a one-tap alternative, terms in plain sight.

Splash

The brand mark and “Style on the Go” — one beat before the app loads.

OTP

Six boxes, a resend timer and nothing else to think about.

UI 02THE HOME SCREEN

A shop window that styles you back.

Categories up top, a stylist’s edit straight after, offers that feel like a perk rather than a pop-up — and a spotlight row that keeps the feed fresh.

1Category tabs

All, Women, Men and Kids in one swipeable row — with a peek of each.

Shop by momentEthnic · Western · Active · Night

Shop by moment, not by department, with tiles that show the vibe.

3Curated by stylist

A stylist’s edit sits second on the page — the human touch, up front.

4Offers as perks

First-order savings appear as a code card, not an interruption.

5Deals for you

Personalised discounts in a two-up grid, easy to scan with a thumb.

6In the spotlight

Featured brands, sports and ethnic edits keep the feed changing.

Easy access

A style community

Inside a stylist’s world
