NYUMBA

Resident app · iOS · Android · Web

● OVERVIEW

Nyumba runs student housing in Kenya. Every month a resident pays rent, tops up prepaid power, chases a repair and lets a friend in at the gate. I designed one resident app — mobile and web — where the most urgent thing is always the first thing you see.

● THE PROBLEM

Four monthly errands.
Four different doors.

Rent and deposit invoices, prepaid electricity, repairs in the room and guests at the gate. Each one had its own steps — a payment here, a token there, a request to the property team somewhere else.

01

Rent & deposits

Invoices, partial payments and refunds — mostly paid over M-Pesa.

02

Prepaid power

Tokens bought for one specific meter, ideally before the lights go out.

03

Repairs in the room

Electrical, plumbing, Wi-Fi, paint — and knowing when it is really fixed.

04

Guests at the gate

Inviting friends ahead of time, so the gate already knows they are coming.

THE BRIEF

“One resident app, on mobile and web, where the most urgent thing is always the first thing you see.”

So the home screen had to answer, at a glance —

01What do I owe?

02Is my power running low?

03Is anything still broken?

● 01 — HOME

One screen that answers
three questions.

The home screen took the most rounds — fifteen-plus versions, narrowed to four, then the final. With each round the amount due crept higher, until it landed in the header beside Pay Now.

1The amount due leads

The one number residents act on sits in the header — Pay Now right beside it. Nothing to scroll past.

2Four shortcuts, always in reach

Community, visitors, electricity and maintenance — one tap each, right under the header.

3Wallet with refunds built in

Balance and Request Refunds share one card, so getting money back isn’t a separate hunt.

4Status cards that peek

Token balance and active tickets sit in cards that peek off the edge — a hint there’s more to swipe.

5Booking at a glance

Building, room, check-in and check-out, with live status on the card.

6Invoices, with their state

Each invoice shows its amount and Paid or Pending, so nothing hides behind a menu.

● 02 — FINANCIALS

Paying,
without the guesswork.

Invoices, payments and refunds live in three tabs, filtered by status, type and date. Paying is a single sheet with as few decisions as possible.

  1. 1Total firstThe sheet opens on the full amount due, before any choice.
  2. 2Full or partialPay the whole amount, or switch to Other Amount and pay part.
  3. 3M-Pesa first, card as fallbackThe methods residents actually use, side by side — phone number prefilled.
  4. 4Amount above the buttonThe final figure repeats right above Pay Now. No last-step surprise.

● 03 — ELECTRICITY

Power, topped up in three taps.

Type an amount or pick a quick one, confirm the M-Pesa number, done. Building, room and meter number come straight from the booking — nothing to look up or mistype.

Paid through M-PesaThe sheet asks for the M-Pesa account first — the way residents already top up.
Quick amountsCommon top-ups are one tap; any other amount can still be typed in KES.
Filled from the bookingBuilding, room and meter come from the booking — nothing to look up.

● 04 — MAINTENANCE

Every ticket gets
its own illustration.

Electrical, plumbing, Wi-Fi, paint, carpentry, fumigation, the bus fleet — each category opens with its own illustrated header, so residents always know which ticket they are in.

TICKET LIFECYCLE

The resident
closes the loop.

A ticket starts with a category, a location, a short description and an optional photo. When the team marks it done, the resident confirms it is solved — or reopens it with a thumbs down.

  • Raise — category, location, photo
  • Track — number, time and status on top
  • Confirm — solved, or reopen it

● 05 — LIFE AROUND THE ROOM

The softer side of living together.

KNOW YOUR COMMUNITY

Find neighbours
who like what you like.

Residents pick interests — sports, arts and more — and see people nearby who share them. Showing up in “nearby” is a toggle, off until the resident turns it on.

  • Cricket
  • Football
  • Cycling
  • Chess
  • Badminton
  • + Add interest

NOTIFICATIONS & NOTICES

Updates for you, notices for everyone.

Booking updates and rent reminders sit apart from building-wide notices, which open as a sheet with any links residents need.

VISITORS

Guests, expected at the gate.

Invite with name, phone, ID and visit time — then follow each visit from invited to checked in and checked out.

● 06 — DESIGN LANGUAGE

A calm system,
built on one green.

One brand green carries every primary action and header. Dark surfaces are kept for money, so the wallet always reads as the most valuable thing on screen.

● 07 — THE WHOLE APP

Every screen.
One resident.

● 08 — WEB PORTAL

Same product, wider screen.

The portal mirrors the app — the same modules in a top navigation, the same cards, the same invoice list. Residents who pay from a laptop don’t have to learn a second product.
